What is Beko?

Beko is the UK division of a global appliance manufacturing powerhouse with decades of heritage. It serves professional buyers in a highly competitive market where cutting-edge products deserve equally credible digital communication. That was the gap: the UK division’s website banners, sales emails and promo assets looked years behind the innovative global brand they represented.

Challenge

The UK division’s digital presence failed to reflect the stature of the global brand, and the credibility gap risked undermining premium products in the eyes of discerning buyers. The small local marketing team was overstretched, lacking capacity and specialist skills for a full digital modernisation plus ongoing asset production. Ad-hoc freelancers were too fragile a solution for a major corporation’s division.

Project Goals

  • Rapidly elevate the professionalism and modernity of all UK-specific digital assets
  • Establish a reliable pipeline for ongoing marketing collateral, campaigns and promotions
  • Communicate complex product benefits clearly to customers and professional audiences

What We Did

Beko UK subscribed to a dedicated creative unit: designers and marketing specialists available four hours every day, working as a flexible external creative department. The strategic focus was to inject modernity and local relevance while keeping global brand recognition untouched.

Polished, high-conversion email templates for promotions, product launches and trade communications, built to render flawlessly across clients.
